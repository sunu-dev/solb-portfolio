Create a premium high fidelity UI art-direction mockup for a Korean beginner stock dictionary named Joobi. User has rejected flat scanned-document-looking UI and amateur book simulation. Show a real tactile small open book integrated into a very restrained modern white web reader, photoreal material quality, not an antique book. Straight-on slightly overhead view, nearly orthographic, fully readable pages. Soft daylight from upper left reveals fine uncoated ivory paper fibers, gentle natural page camber near the binding, subtle stacked thin page edges along bottom and fore-edge, a narrow muted sage-green cloth cover peeking out. Real delicate paper, not yellow parchment, no stains, no rough noisy overlay. Elegant modern Korean sans-serif typography like Pretendard, never handwriting or serif. Two facing pages: left heading exact Korean '주식', right heading '주가'. Small top UI title exact '내 주식사전', small search control '단어 찾기'. Left short text '회사의 일부를 소유하는 권리예요.' Right short text '주식 한 주를 사고파는 가격이에요.' Use sensible editorial spacing and modest type sizes; book compact, not giant blank cards. Right page's lower outer corner lifts and curls naturally about 20%, thin paper transmits a hint of reverse print, soft realistic occlusion shadow, showing actual supple curved sheet rather than a rigid diagonal board. Minimal bottom previous/next chevrons and small page number 1–2 / 58. No mascot, no decorative slogan, no mockup device frame, no perspective tilt of the whole screen, no desk props, no excessive rounded panels. Landscape 3:2 composition.
